BeeHive Spell · Privacy Policy

Your child's progress stays on your device

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Developer: Hounien Lin ("we")

Contact email: hounienlin@gmail.com

BeeHive Spell (拼字蜂巢, "the app") helps elementary-school children practice English spelling. Our design principle is simple: we do not collect any personal information. This page explains, plainly, what the app actually does with data (mostly: nothing).

01We do not collect personal information

  • The app does not need an account and has no login.
  • The app has no server of our own and does not upload data anywhere.
  • We do not collect, store, or send any child's or parent's name, date of birth, email, phone number, location, photos, voice, device identifiers, or usage history.
  • The app has no ads and uses no third-party SDKs (no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools).

02Data that stays on your device

To make the app work, the following is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot access it:

  • Children's nicknames (up to 4, created by a parent in the app)
  • Learning progress: cells played, stars, titles, Review Basket, honey
  • Settings: app language, weekly goal, sounds and effects, map style
  • Words and roads ("My Roads") added by a parent

Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

03Photographing a word list: camera and photos

  • The app uses the camera only after a grown-up passes the parental gate and chooses to photograph a school word list.
  • Words are read from the photo on this device only, using Apple's built-in text recognition. The photo is not saved and is not uploaded.
  • When you pick photos from your library, the app uses the iOS system photo picker and can only see the photos you pick. They are also processed only on the device.
  • You can turn off camera access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera. You can still add words from a photo in your library or by typing.

04Parental gate

Purchases, Restore Purchases, settings, adding or deleting a child, adding words, sharing or importing a road, and any external link all require a grown-up to answer a math question first.

05Sharing a road

"Share a Road" creates a .json file that contains only the word list and the road name. It contains no progress, stars, nicknames, or photos. You send the file yourself through the iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop or Messages); it never passes through us.

06Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase has been made so it can unlock content. For refunds and purchase history, please contact Apple.

07Information provided by Apple

If you choose "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy, and we cannot use it to identify you or your child.

08Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. Because we do not collect personal information, we never ask children for it.

09Deleting data

We do not keep any of your data, so there is no server data to delete. To delete data on your device, delete a child in the Parent Area, or delete the app.

10Changes to this policy

If a change to the app affects privacy, we will first update this policy and its "last updated" date, and mention it in the app's update notes. We will never change it quietly.

11Contact us

If you have any questions, email hounienlin@gmail.com. A real person will reply.

About this website: this is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no tracking code and no analytics, and it loads no third-party fonts or resources. As the host, GitHub may log connection data (such as IP addresses) under its own policies; we do not receive or use that data.
