BeeHive Spell · Free and Pro

Learn 300 words free, then decide

We want parents to see their child enjoy the app before paying anything. So the free version is not a few-day trial and nothing is hidden: every feature works until your child has learned 300 words.

02Free: how the 300 words are counted

  • We count the different words your child has learned, both built-in words and words you add. Deleted custom words no longer count.
  • A word is learned when it is answered correctly in spelling practice, the Spelling Contest, Look-Cover-Write, Letter Scramble, Missing Letter or Choose the Spelling. Listening-only games (Memory Match, Listen and Choose, Listen and Match) don't count.
  • Until 300 learned words, no purchase screen ever appears.

03After 300 learned words

Each time practice starts, the Pro screen appears first, for a parent to decide. Tap Cancel and everything stays as it is: nothing is unlocked and nothing is charged. Mimi never pushes kids to buy; purchases only appear where a grown-up is needed.

04What Pro opens

  • No more learned-word limit
  • All 3,000 words and the whole 300-cell map
  • Every Hive Challenge puzzle
  • Grade contest word packs: K–2, 3–5, 6–8 and 9–12
  • No limit on new words in My Roads

Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase: buy it once and never pay again. No subscription, no auto-renewal.

05How to buy

Parent Area > Purchases and restore > Buy Pro (full version), or tap Buy Pro (full version) on the Pro screen. Both need a grown-up to answer a math question first. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

06Restoring a purchase

  • Parent Area > Purchases and restore > Restore purchases
  • Or tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen

Both are behind the parental gate. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Restoring never charges you again.

Family Sharing: Pro supports Family Sharing. Members of your Family Sharing group sign in with their own Apple Account and tap Restore purchases in the Parent Area to unlock Pro, without buying it again.

07New device or reinstall

  1. Install BeeHive Spell on the new device, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.
  2. Open the Parent Area and answer the math question.
  3. In Purchases and restore, tap Restore purchases. Pro opens again.

Progress currently stays on the original device and does not move with Pro. You can export your own roads on the old device and import them on the new one. See Support.
