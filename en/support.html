BeeHive Spell · Support and FAQ

Stuck? Write to us

BeeHive Spell is made by one developer. If something goes wrong, a word looks odd, or you have an idea, just email. A real person reads and replies.

Email hounienlin@gmail.com

It helps to include your device (iPhone or iPad), iOS version and a screenshot. Please don't include your child's full name or other personal details.

01Where is the Parent Area?

It is the last tab. Answer a math question to enter. Purchases, Restore Purchases, settings, children, adding words and sharing roads are all inside.

02How do I restore my purchase?

There are two places, both behind the parental gate:

  • Parent Area > Purchases and restore > Restore purchases
  • On the Pro screen, tap Restore purchases

Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Pro is a one-time purchase, so restoring never charges you again.

03Does Pro or progress move to a new device?

  • Pro does: install BeeHive Spell on the new device, use the same Apple Account, and tap Restore purchases in the Parent Area.
  • Progress does not, for now: stars, titles, honey, the Review Basket and your own roads stay on the original device. To bring your own roads along, go to Parent Area > Custom road backups > Export all custom roads on the old device, then send the .json file to the new device and import it (the file holds only road names, levels and words).

04How do the 300 free words work?

  • We count the different words your child has learned, both built-in words and words you add. Deleted custom words no longer count.
  • Listening-only games (Memory Match, Listen and Choose, Listen and Match) don't count as learning. Correct answers in spelling practice, the Spelling Contest, Look-Cover-Write, Letter Scramble, Missing Letter and Choose the Spelling do.
  • Until 300 learned words, every feature is available and no purchase screen appears.
  • After 300 learned words, starting practice shows the Pro screen first, for a parent to decide. Cancel keeps everything as it is.

Full details: Free and Pro.

05How do I delete data?

We have no server and keep none of your data, so there is only data on your device:

  • To delete one child's data: Parent Area > Children > Delete (behind the parental gate).
  • To delete everything: delete the app.

Your purchase is kept by Apple, so deleting the app does not affect it. After reinstalling, tap Restore purchases to get Pro back.

06Are word-list photos uploaded?

No. Text is recognized on this device only. Photos are never saved or uploaded. You can also turn off camera access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and pick photos or type words instead.

07How do I share a road with classmates?

My Roads > pick a road > Share (behind the parental gate), then send the .json file with AirDrop, Messages or similar. The file holds only the road name, levels and words: no progress, names or photos.

08How do I ask for a refund?

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We can't see payment details or issue refunds ourselves. Please request one at Apple's Report a Problem page (reportaproblem.apple.com).

09Feedback

A meaning or pronunciation that looks wrong, something awkward, a game you'd love to see: email hounienlin@gmail.com. Your feedback shapes the next version.
